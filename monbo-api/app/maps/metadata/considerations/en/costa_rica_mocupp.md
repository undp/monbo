### Data definitions and Limitacions

MOCUPP measures tree cover loss, gain, or no change for Costa Rica.

**Tree cover**: natural, semi-natural, or planted areas, mainly composed of tree or shrub elements (native or exotic species), identifiable at a 1:10,000 scale, using a 10x10 meter pixel. The minimum mapping unit (MMU) is 0.5 ha, covered by either open or closed canopy. It includes forest in all its successional stages, mangroves, paramo, and forest plantations. Additionally, yolillales and bamboo stands are incorporated, since at the applied scale and resolution, these covers resemble tree or shrub elements (Aguilar, Vargas, Ávila & Miller, 2020).

### Treen Cover loss and gain detection

**Treen Cover loss detection**: Removal of tree cover and the emergence of new areas of the productive landscape. That can be distinguished at a 1:10,000 scale using a 10x10 meter pixel.

**Treen Cover gain detection**: Increase in tree cover. That can be distinguished at a 1:10,000 scale using a 10x10 meter pixel. Includes: natural regeneration, assisted regeneration, or reforestation.
