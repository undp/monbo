## Requirements
### Requirement: Single start page at the locale root

The frontend SHALL serve the start page at `/[locale]`. It SHALL show, in this order, a hero, the "Así funciona" band with three step cards, a start bar to choose the country and begin, and a trust row. It SHALL NOT require a selected country to render.

#### Scenario: First visit

- **WHEN** a user in a new tab opens `/`
- **THEN** the page shows the hero, the three step cards and the start bar, with Colombia preselected

#### Scenario: English

- **WHEN** the user opens `/en`
- **THEN** every text of the start page is in English

### Requirement: Dark header on the start page only

On the start page the header SHALL be drawn over the hero, without a solid background, with the white version of the logo and its navigation and language menu in white. It SHALL NOT show the country selector on the start page. Every other page SHALL keep the light header with its country selector.

#### Scenario: Start page header

- **WHEN** the user is on `/`
- **THEN** the header shows the white logo, the PNUD mark, the navigation links and the language menu over the hero, and no country selector

#### Scenario: Other pages unchanged

- **WHEN** the user goes from `/` to `/polygons-validation/upload-data`
- **THEN** the header has its light background and shows the selected country

### Requirement: Hero stresses free access

The hero SHALL show, under the header, three badges: "Gratis y de libre acceso" (highlighted), "Sin registro" and "No guardamos tus polígonos". It SHALL show the eyebrow "Monbo · Producción sostenible", the title "Revisa tu producción en 3 pasos." and a lead sentence that ends, on the same line when the width allows it, with a "Cómo funciona ↗" link to the methodology page (`/documentation`). The hero background SHALL combine a dark navy-to-forest gradient with an aerial image of forest, a river and farm plots bundled with the app. The image is decorative: it SHALL have no accessible name. The page SHALL NOT mention "mapas disponibles".

#### Scenario: Badges and title

- **WHEN** the start page renders in Spanish
- **THEN** the hero shows the three badges and the title "Revisa tu producción en 3 pasos."

#### Scenario: How it works

- **WHEN** the user clicks "Cómo funciona ↗"
- **THEN** the browser navigates to the methodology page in the current language

### Requirement: Three step cards

The "Así funciona" band SHALL show the subtitle "Empieza por el paso 1. Si ya tienes tus polígonos validados, puedes ir directo al 2." and three numbered cards joined by arrows:

1. "Valida tus polígonos": pressing the card SHALL open stage 1 (polygon validation). The card SHALL also offer "Descargar plantilla", which SHALL download the upload template for the current language without leaving the page.
2. "Análisis territorial": pressing the card SHALL open stage 2 (layer analysis). The card SHALL show the chips "Deforestación", "Áreas protegidas" and "Frontera agrícola", which SHALL NOT depend on the selected country, and a "Ver capas por país" link to the methodology page.
3. "Descarga tu reporte": the card SHALL NOT be pressable. It SHALL offer "Ver ejemplo de reporte", which SHALL open, in a new tab, a sample PDF report in the current language, generated by the app from the example farms in `public/files/examples/` without the satellite background (no third-party imagery), and served as a static file.

When farms are already loaded, opening stage 1 or 2 from its card SHALL ask, as the module cards did, whether to continue with the same data or upload new data. Every card action SHALL be reachable and usable with the keyboard.

#### Scenario: Open stage 1 without data

- **WHEN** no farms are loaded and the user presses step card 1
- **THEN** the browser navigates to `/polygons-validation/upload-data`

#### Scenario: Open stage 2 directly

- **WHEN** no farms are loaded and the user presses step card 2
- **THEN** the browser navigates to `/territorial-analysis/upload-data`

#### Scenario: Open a stage with data loaded

- **WHEN** farms are loaded and the user presses step card 1
- **THEN** a modal offers "Continuar con los mismos datos" and "Subir nuevos datos"

#### Scenario: Download the template

- **WHEN** the language is English and the user activates "Descargar plantilla" ("Download template")
- **THEN** the browser downloads `/files/m1-upload-file-template-en.xlsx` and stays on the start page

#### Scenario: Example report

- **WHEN** the language is English and the user activates "See a sample report"
- **THEN** the English sample report PDF opens in a new tab and the start page stays open

#### Scenario: Step 3 is not pressable

- **WHEN** the user clicks the body of step card 3
- **THEN** nothing happens and the page does not change

#### Scenario: Category chips are generic

- **WHEN** the user switches the country from Colombia to Costa Rica
- **THEN** step card 2 still shows the same three chips

### Requirement: Start bar with country pills

Under the step cards the page SHALL show a start bar with the label "¿Dónde produces?", one pill per country from `GET /countries` showing the country's name in the current language and its silhouette, a "Empezar en {país}" button, and a "¿Tu país no está? Escríbenos" link. The silhouettes SHALL be bundled with the app and SHALL NOT be fetched from a third party at run time. The selected pill SHALL be highlighted, and exactly one pill SHALL be selected at a time. "Empezar en {país}" SHALL open stage 1 exactly as step card 1 does. The "¿Tu país no está? Escríbenos" link SHALL open the value of `NEXT_PUBLIC_CONTACT_URL` and SHALL NOT be shown when it is empty or unset.

#### Scenario: Start in Ecuador

- **WHEN** the user picks the Ecuador pill and presses "Empezar en Ecuador"
- **THEN** Ecuador is the selected country and the browser opens stage 1

#### Scenario: No contact URL

- **WHEN** `NEXT_PUBLIC_CONTACT_URL` is not set
- **THEN** the start bar shows no "¿Tu país no está? Escríbenos" link

### Requirement: Trust row

Under the start bar the page SHALL show three short statements, each with a check icon: "Desarrollado por el PNUD", "Capas globales y oficiales de cada país" and "Disponible en español e inglés". The row SHALL be hidden below 900 px wide, as in the mockup. It is not part of the content that must fit without scrolling.

#### Scenario: Desktop

- **WHEN** the start page is opened at 1366×768 in Spanish
- **THEN** the three statements are shown under the start bar

### Requirement: Changing the country leaves the steps alone

Choosing another country on the start page SHALL update only the pills and the start button's label. The step cards SHALL NOT re-render and SHALL NOT replay their entrance animation.

#### Scenario: Switch country

- **WHEN** the start page has finished its entrance animation and the user picks the Costa Rica pill
- **THEN** the button reads "Empezar en Costa Rica", the Costa Rica pill is highlighted, and the step cards are the same DOM nodes, not rendered again and not animated again

### Requirement: Fits a 1366×768 screen and adapts to phones

At a 1366×768 viewport the hero, the three step cards and the "Empezar en {país}" button SHALL be fully visible without scrolling. On a taller screen the page SHALL fill its height: the hero SHALL grow into the spare height with its content centered, so that no blank band is left under the trust row. Below 900 px wide the step cards SHALL stack in one column and the start bar SHALL wrap so that no content overflows horizontally. The entrance animation SHALL be skipped when the user prefers reduced motion.

#### Scenario: Laptop screen

- **WHEN** the start page is opened at a 1366×768 viewport
- **THEN** the three step cards and the "Empezar en …" button are visible without scrolling

#### Scenario: Tall screen

- **WHEN** the start page is opened at a 1920×1080 viewport
- **THEN** the hero is taller than at 1366×768, the trust row ends near the bottom of the screen, and no blank band is left under it

#### Scenario: Phone

- **WHEN** the start page is opened at a 390×844 viewport
- **THEN** the step cards are stacked, the page has no horizontal scroll, and every action is reachable by scrolling down

### Requirement: The sample reports link GeoJSON to the production API

The sample reports the start page opens (`/files/examples/es/ejemplo-reporte.pdf` and `/files/examples/en/example-report.pdf`) SHALL link each farm sheet's "Descargar GeoJSON" / "Download GeoJSON" to the production API's `/download-geojson`, whose base URL is the `SAMPLE_REPORTS_API_URL` environment variable (in CI, the repository variable of the same name), not a value written in the code. Each link SHALL carry, in its `content` parameter, a FeatureCollection with exactly one feature, the farm of that sheet, and the five sample farms SHALL each have one link. All the GeoJSON links SHALL share one `https` origin, and no link in the sample reports SHALL point at `localhost`. The web unit tests SHALL check the links against `SAMPLE_REPORTS_API_URL` whenever it is set, and SHALL fail in CI when it is not set.

#### Scenario: A sample farm's GeoJSON link

- **WHEN** a reader of the English sample report opens "Download GeoJSON" on farm E1-003's sheet
- **THEN** the browser requests the production API's `/download-geojson` and receives a FeatureCollection with the single feature E1-003

#### Scenario: No local or non-production links

- **WHEN** the sample reports are regenerated with the GeoJSON link pointing at `localhost` or at an API other than `SAMPLE_REPORTS_API_URL`
- **THEN** the web unit tests fail

#### Scenario: Production's API URL changes

- **WHEN** the repository variable `SAMPLE_REPORTS_API_URL` is changed and the sample reports are not regenerated
- **THEN** the web job's tests fail in CI until both PDFs are regenerated with the new URL

#### Scenario: CI without the variable

- **WHEN** the web unit tests run with `CI` set and `SAMPLE_REPORTS_API_URL` unset
- **THEN** they fail, naming the missing variable
