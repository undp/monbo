## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Three step cards

The "Así funciona" band SHALL show the subtitle "Empieza por el paso 1. Si ya tienes tus polígonos validados, puedes ir directo al 2." and three numbered cards joined by arrows:

1. "Valida tus polígonos": pressing the card SHALL open stage 1 (polygon validation). The card SHALL also offer "Descargar plantilla", which SHALL download the upload template for the current language without leaving the page.
2. "Análisis territorial": pressing the card SHALL open stage 2 (layer analysis). The card SHALL show the chips "Deforestación", "Áreas protegidas" and "Frontera agrícola", which SHALL NOT depend on the selected country, and a "Ver capas por país" link to the methodology page.
3. "Descarga tu reporte": the card SHALL NOT be pressable. It SHALL offer "Ver ejemplo de reporte", which SHALL open, in a new tab, a sample PDF report in the current language, generated by the app from the example farms in `public/files/examples/` without the satellite background (no third-party imagery), and served as a static file.

When farms are already loaded, opening stage 1 or 2 from its card SHALL ask, as the module cards did, whether to continue with the same data or upload new data. Every card action SHALL be reachable and usable with the keyboard.

#### Scenario: Open stage 1 without data

- **WHEN** no farms are loaded and the user presses step card 1
- **THEN** the browser navigates to `/polygons-validation/upload-data`

#### Scenario: Open stage 2 directly

- **WHEN** no farms are loaded and the user presses step card 2
- **THEN** the browser navigates to `/territorial-analysis/upload-data`

#### Scenario: Open a stage with data loaded

- **WHEN** farms are loaded and the user presses step card 1
- **THEN** a modal offers "Continuar con los mismos datos" and "Subir nuevos datos"

#### Scenario: Download the template

- **WHEN** the language is English and the user activates "Descargar plantilla" ("Download template")
- **THEN** the browser downloads `/files/m1-upload-file-template-en.xlsx` and stays on the start page

#### Scenario: Example report

- **WHEN** the language is English and the user activates "See a sample report"
- **THEN** the English sample report PDF opens in a new tab and the start page stays open

#### Scenario: Step 3 is not pressable

- **WHEN** the user clicks the body of step card 3
- **THEN** nothing happens and the page does not change

#### Scenario: Category chips are generic

- **WHEN** the user switches the country from Colombia to Costa Rica
- **THEN** step card 2 still shows the same three chips
