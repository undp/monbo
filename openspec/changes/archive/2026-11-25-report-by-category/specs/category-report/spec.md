## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Results are classified with the layer's category

The report and the exports SHALL classify each farm's result for a layer with the rule of the layer's category (its polarity and its resolved threshold from `GET /categories`), through the web's one classification function. The text of a result SHALL be the category's `resultLabels.ok` or `resultLabels.flagged` in the report's language, and "Sin dato" / "No data" when the result is `unknown`. The percentage shown SHALL be the finding: `value` for an `avoid` category and `1 − value` for a `required` one. A layer whose category is not among the country's categories SHALL have every result shown as "Sin dato" / "No data", never classified with another category's rule.

#### Scenario: Required category

- **WHEN** a farm's result for an agricultural-frontier layer (`required`, threshold 5) is `0.82`
- **THEN** its finding is 18 % and it is shown with the category's flagged label

#### Scenario: Avoid category under its threshold

- **WHEN** a farm's result for a deforestation layer (`avoid`, threshold 2) is `0.01`
- **THEN** it is shown with the category's ok label and a finding of 1 %

#### Scenario: Unknown category

- **WHEN** a selected layer's `categoryId` is not in the country's categories
- **THEN** its results are shown as "No data", and no ok or flagged label is printed for them

### Requirement: The cover shows no results

The report's cover SHALL be titled "Reporte de análisis territorial" / "Territorial analysis report" and SHALL show the association, the country of origin, the report date and the number of polygons. It SHALL include a "This report includes" block listing each category of the selected layers with its name, colour and number of selected layers, and an index of the report's sections linked to them inside the PDF. The cover SHALL NOT show any percentage, result class or result label.

#### Scenario: CO with three categories

- **WHEN** a report is generated for 4 farms of CO with two deforestation layers, one protected-areas layer and one agricultural-frontier layer
- **THEN** the cover lists "Deforestación · 2 capas", "Áreas protegidas · 1 capa" and "Frontera agrícola · 1 capa", says 4 polygons, and shows no percentage or result

#### Scenario: Index

- **WHEN** the user clicks "Metodología" in the cover's index
- **THEN** the PDF viewer goes to the methodology page

### Requirement: One sheet per farm grouped by category

For each selected farm, the report SHALL include a sheet with the farm's data (ID, producer, crop, production, production date, country and region, area), its GeoJSON link and its documents, followed by its results grouped by category in the order of `GET /categories`. Each category SHALL show its name, its colour and its overall class: `flagged` when any of its layers is flagged, otherwise `unknown` when any of its layers is unknown, otherwise `ok`. Under it, each selected layer of that category SHALL be one row with the layer's name and period, the finding percentage and the result label. A farm SHALL have its sheet even when all its results are `null`.

#### Scenario: A null result

- **WHEN** a farm's result for one layer is `null` and the others have values
- **THEN** its sheet lists that layer with "Sin dato" and no percentage, and the category containing it is not shown as without findings unless another layer in it is flagged

#### Scenario: Every result null

- **WHEN** every result of a farm is `null`
- **THEN** the report still includes that farm's sheet, with every layer as "Sin dato"

#### Scenario: Category summary

- **WHEN** a farm has no findings in two of three categories
- **THEN** its sheet says "2 de 3 categorías sin observaciones"

### Requirement: Map pages per layer with a result

After each farm's sheet, the report SHALL include one map page per selected layer for which the farm has a result, titled with the layer's category and name and the result label, with the map image generated by the API. A layer whose result is `null` SHALL have no map page. The caption about satellite imagery SHALL appear only when the map has the satellite background.

#### Scenario: Without satellite background

- **WHEN** the report has more farms than the satellite limit, so its maps use the solid background
- **THEN** no map page mentions satellite imagery

#### Scenario: With satellite background

- **WHEN** the maps include the satellite background
- **THEN** each map page shows the satellite imagery caption under its map

### Requirement: Generic methodology page

The report SHALL include one methodology page that explains, without naming deforestation as the only analysis: the formula (share of the polygon's area that coincides with the layer), what the finding is for an `avoid` and a `required` category, that a result has findings when the finding is above the category's threshold, and that "No data" means the result could not be computed. It SHALL list each category of the selected layers with its description, polarity reading, threshold and both result labels, and each selected layer with its category, source, period and resolution (its coverage is on its scope and limits page).

#### Scenario: Required category explained

- **WHEN** the report includes an agricultural-frontier category
- **THEN** the methodology page says that for it the finding is the share of the polygon outside the layer, and shows its threshold and its two labels

### Requirement: Scope and limits per layer

The report SHALL include, for every selected layer, a page with its category, its attributes (coverage, source, resolution, content date, update frequency, publish date, references) and its considerations, in the report's language. A layer SHALL have its page even when every result for it is `null`.

#### Scenario: Layer with no results

- **WHEN** every farm's result for a selected layer is `null`
- **THEN** the report still includes that layer's scope and limits page

### Requirement: Per-farm reports and large reports keep the same content

Each PDF in the ZIP of separated reports SHALL contain the same sections as the complete report, for its one farm. The large-report rule SHALL count the farm sheets as well as the map pages (see `report-generation`).

#### Scenario: ZIP

- **WHEN** the user downloads the separated reports for 3 farms
- **THEN** each PDF has the cover, that farm's sheet and map pages, the methodology and the scope and limits pages
